About British Tennis Players

This page brings together British players’ world rankings and their tournament entries for this week, next week and the following week.

  • Men: ATP Tour and ATP Challenger events only, including qualifying entries.
  • Women: WTA Tour events and ITF World Tennis Tour events at W50 level and above, including qualifying entries.
  • Explore the schedule: switch between Men and Women, choose a week, search for a player or tournament, filter by ranking, and sort the list.

Rankings and schedules are based on the data supplied for this page and have not been independently verified. Tournament entries can change, so check the relevant tournament source for the latest confirmation.

About this site

British Tennis Players is an independent fan-run project designed to make it easier to follow British players on the professional tennis tours. It brings together a searchable player list and a three-week tournament schedule in one place.

The site is not affiliated with or endorsed by the ATP, WTA, ITF or Lawn Tennis Association. Tournament entries, rankings and player availability can change; please check the official tour or tournament source for confirmation.

We aim to make the information useful and easy to navigate, and to improve the site as the project develops.
